## Filling guide, What is known about it

How the findings rows of the fourth section are filled. The guide is for the pipeline and for anyone generating a card by hand or with a model. It is not part of the card. A row holds one value per source that speaks, the author's files, a registry, a check, the assembler's model or a reviewer, each with its marker, and values that disagree all stand. An empty value is always written as unknown, not examined, none or not declared, never left blank. When no check covered a field, the check value reads not examined. The Checks and Evaluations tables are filled from the tool's output and the run record, one row per check or run, with each column as the section defines it, and a producer's own tests of its output are listed in neither. Every risk entry has the same five parts, so that risks from the author, from a checker, from the model and from a reviewer read alike and differ only in their source. The taxonomy linked is the IBM AI Risk Atlas, with the Risk Atlas Nexus used for mapping to other taxonomies, pending confirmation.

| Field | How to fill |
|---|---|
| vulnerabilities | Fed only by the Checks table. One entry per finding with the tool and its rule or identifier, the category as the tool names it, severity and confidence as the tool recorded them, file and line, the status as open, fixed in a named version, accepted with a reason, false positive or disputed, and the suggested fix as the tool recorded it. A status other than open is set by a reviewer or by the pipeline when a later version no longer shows the finding. The author never writes here, a weakness the files mention is a limitation or a risk. Record not examined when no check ran, and none when checks ran and found nothing, naming the checks. When any check ran, add a one-line summary of the findings, computed. |
| risks | A risk is a potential harm to the user, their data, their system or third parties from using the skill. A caution on how to perform a step correctly is an instruction, and a statement that a result may be wrong or incomplete is a limitation, so neither is a risk. One entry per risk with five parts. The risk, a short name. The description, one or two sentences that say what could happen and under which conditions. The taxonomy, a link to the matching entry of the IBM AI Risk Atlas where one fits, otherwise none, with the mapping marked model-generated when the model made it. The source, the author with file and line, a checker with its check id, the pipeline's model with the rows of the card the risk derives from, or a reviewer with role and date. The mitigation, what mitigates the risk and whether the files say it is in place, or none stated. Severity is recorded only as a source rated it, since a label such as critical on an instruction is not a rating, and the card never assigns one. The Author value takes risks from passages in the files that warn of harm, and reads not declared when there are none. A check or a risk tool contributes its own entries with a pointer into its report, and not examined when none covered risks. The pipeline's model proposes at most five risks from what the card already records, the credentials, network access, side effects and cost rows, whether or not a check ran, each anchored to those rows and carrying the marker model-generated with the model and the prompt version. When any check ran, add a one-line summary, computed. No overall score is computed. |
| limitations | One entry per limitation, taken from passages in the files, including messages that scripts print, that state what the skill cannot do, where it fails or degrades, or what it needs that may be absent, with file and line, or from a run that observed a failure, with a pointer into the check's report. Advice to avoid a technique for large inputs counts as a limitation. Then one entry per known problem the files describe, whether headed troubleshooting, known issues or common problems or placed inside a workflow, with the problem and the way around it as the files state them, with file and line, and a summary of a long passage marked model-generated. Record not declared when the files state none, and not examined when no run took place. What the card's assembly did not do is recorded in the card stamp, not here. |
