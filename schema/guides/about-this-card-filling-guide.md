## Filling guide, About this card

How the closing section is filled. The guide is for the pipeline and for anyone generating a card by hand or with a model. It is not part of the card. The card stamp is computed by the assembler and carries one value.

| Field | How to fill |
|---|---|
| card stamp | Record the version label of this schema that the card follows, for example v1.2 of 22 October 2026. Record who assembled the card, the pipeline with its version, a model with its model identifier and prompt version, or the name of the person, and the date of assembly. Record what the assembly did not do, as a fixed list answered with yes or no, whether the skill was run, whether its scripts were analysed, whether a security check ran, whether an external service was contacted, and whether a registry was read. The stamp changes every time the card is assembled again, for example after a check has been rerun. |
